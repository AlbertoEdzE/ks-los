# Phase 7 Integration Notes (LOS v2)

## Objective

Phase 7 integrates go-live readiness practices: governance, ethics, compliance, and operational runbooks aligned to a production-like rollout.

---

## Components Integrated

1. **Governance**
   - RBAC policy enforcement
   - Audit retention and review workflows
   - Catalog and policy change management

2. **Compliance**
   - Consent tracking and retention controls
   - Evidence-based outputs for recommendations and decisions

3. **Fairness and Ethics**
   - Bias and parity checks
   - Drift policies and human review triggers

4. **Operations**
   - Incident response and rollback runbooks
   - Backup/restore procedures and drills
